
## Read all flash contents from ATmega32 to a hex file:
avrdude -c usbasp -p m32 -B 10 -U flash:r:chip_contents_read.hex:i

## Test connection / probe ATmega32:
avrdude -c usbasp -p m32 -B 10 -v

## Flash compiled hex to ATmega32:
avrdude -c usbasp -p m32 -B 10 -U flash:w:main.hex:i

## Erase chip:
avrdude -c usbasp -p m32 -B 10 -e
